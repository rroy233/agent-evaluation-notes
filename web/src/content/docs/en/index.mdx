## The five-chapter thread

Each chapter solves a different problem and produces a different artifact: Chapter 1 produces success criteria, Chapter 2 turns them into tasks, Chapter 3 gives grading rules, Chapter 4 makes results comparable, and Chapter 5 feeds production signals back into the task set.
